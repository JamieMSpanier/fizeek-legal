Delete Your Account

Effective Date: [set on publication] (DRAFT)

Draft for review, not in force.

You can delete your Fizeek account and its data at any time, from the app, from the coaching website, or by email. This page says how, what is deleted, and what is kept.

01

In the App

Open Fizeek and tap Profile. Tap the pencil icon in the top right to open Edit Profile, scroll to Account, and tap Delete Account. Read the warning, tap Continue, type DELETE, then tap Delete Forever.

Your account is deleted straight away. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it. If you want a copy of anything, request your data first.

02

On the Coaching Website

Coaches: sign in, open Settings, and in the Account card tap Delete Account, then Delete Forever. Settings stays open to you even when your account is limited to Billing.

Clients: sign in and open the page where you pay your coach. In its Account card tap Delete Account, then Delete Forever. If you do not have a coach, delete your account in the app.

Deleting on the website does exactly what deleting in the app does.

03

By Email

If you cannot get into the app or the website, email support@fizeekapp.com from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. From another address we will need other proof that the account is yours. We reply within 30 days, and the result is the same as deleting it yourself.

04

Payments Stop First

Before anything is deleted, we cancel every subscription the account has at Stripe: what you pay a coach, your coach plan if you are a coach, and what your clients pay you. Coaching ends at once, with no refund. If a cancellation fails, nothing is deleted and you can try again. Fizeek Pro bought through Apple or Google is managed in your app store subscriptions.

05

What Is Deleted

Your account and profile: your login, email address, username, name, date of birth, bio, profile photo, banner and notification token.

Your training: every workout and template, your routines, your workout history and every set, cardio, personal records, trends, body weight, goals and achievements.

Your coaching, if you are a client: your coaching links, check-ins and answers with their photos and videos, your lab results, and everything your coach made for you. Your former coach no longer sees any of it.

Your social activity: posts, comments, likes, follows and your blocked list.

Your messages: every one-to-one conversation you are part of is deleted for both people, with every message either of you sent and everything sent with them. Your messages in group chats are removed from the group.

Your photos and files: every image you uploaded is deleted from our storage. A group chat's photo stays with the group.

Also: your data exports, your notifications and your search history.

06

If You Are a Coach

Each of your coaching relationships ends at once. Each client keeps everything that was part of their coaching, because it is their record: their check-ins and answers with your replies and the videos on them, their check-in forms, their lab results, their training program, meal plan, cardio and supplement plans, a copy of the routine and meal plan as you last left them, their phases, the notes you shared with them, their calendar, the record of changes to their plans, their calls and their scores. None of it keeps your name or account attached.

Your one-to-one conversation with each person is deleted for both of you. What was only yours is deleted: your private notes about clients, your settings, the forms and videos no client was given, and your billing records with your clients.

07

What We Keep

A few records stay, with your name, email address and account ID removed: feedback you sent us, any group chat you created (so its members keep their conversation), and any custom exercise you made (switched off, so lifts other people logged with it keep a name).

Payment records are kept for tax and legal reasons: subscription and purchase events, coaching payments, ambassador referrals and payments we made. Our copies of the payment messages Stripe and the app stores send us still contain your account ID or email address.

Deleted data can remain in our database provider's encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten. Email support@fizeekapp.com if you want your feedback or a group chat you created removed too.

08

Copies Held by Other Companies

Some services that help run Fizeek keep their own copy of a small amount of data, and deleting your account does not reach those copies: Sentry (crash reports, which can include workout values), PostHog (a small set of usage events with your account ID and email), RevenueCat (your purchase and subscription history) and Stripe (the record of coaching payments, kept by your coach's Stripe account, or by yours if you are a coach).

Email support@fizeekapp.com to have your Sentry crash reports and PostHog events deleted too. Your purchase history at RevenueCat is kept for tax and legal reasons.

09

Uninstalling Is Not Deleting

Removing Fizeek from your phone does not delete your account. Your data stays and you can sign back in. Use one of the routes above to delete it.

10

Questions

Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why. If anything here is unclear, or you are not sure your account was deleted, get in touch.
