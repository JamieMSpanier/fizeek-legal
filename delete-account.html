Delete Your Account

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Draft for review, not in force. This version has not been reviewed by a lawyer.

This page explains how to delete your Fizeek account and what happens to your data when you do. Fizeek is published by Fizeek LLC. You can delete your account in the app, on the Fizeek coaching website, or by emailing us.

01

Delete your account in the app

Anyone can delete their account from the app. It takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Fizeek and tap Profile in the bottom bar.
  2. Tap the pencil icon in the top right to open Edit Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom, to the section headed Account.
  4. Tap Delete Account, the last row, shown in red.
  5. Read the warning and tap Continue.
  6. Type DELETE in the box to confirm, then tap Delete Forever.

Your account and your data are deleted straight away. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it. If you want a copy of anything, save it before you start.

02

Delete your account on the coaching website

If you are a coach, sign in to the coaching website, open Settings and find the Account card. Tap Delete Account and confirm with Delete Forever. Settings and its Account card stay open to you even when your account is limited to Billing because a payment did not go through.

If you are a client, sign in to the coaching website and open the page where you pay your coach. Its Account card has Delete Account; confirm with Delete Forever. If you do not have a coach, delete your account from the app instead.

Deleting on the website does exactly what deleting in the app does.

03

Delete your account by email

If you cannot get into the app or the website, email us at support@fizeekapp.com and ask us to delete your account.

Please send the email from the email address on the account. That is how we confirm the request is really from you. If you send it from a different address we will have to ask you for something else that proves the account is yours, which slows things down.

We will reply within 30 days. That is our commitment on data requests, including deletion requests, and it matches Section 13 of our Privacy Policy. Once we delete the account, the effect is exactly the same as deleting it yourself.

04

Payments stop first

Before anything is deleted, we cancel at Stripe every subscription the account has: what you pay a coach, your own Fizeek coach plan if you are a coach, and what each of your clients pays you. If a cancellation fails, nothing is deleted, and you can try again.

05

What gets deleted

Deleting your account removes all of the following from our database and our file storage, along with your data exports, your notifications and your search history.

Your account and profile

Your login, your email address, your username, your name, your date of birth, your bio, your profile photo, your banner image, your push notification token, and any answers you gave during sign up.

Your training data

Every workout and template you built, your splits, your full workout history, every set with its weight and reps, your cardio entries, your personal records, your progress trends, your body weight entries, your goals, your achievements, your setup cues, and any request you sent us to set up a split for you.

Your coaching, if you are a client

Your coaching links, your check-ins and your answers to them, the videos and photos you sent with them, and everything your coach made for you: your plans, notes, schedules, supplements and lab work.

Your social activity

Your posts, your comments, your likes, who you follow and who follows you, your favorite friends, and your blocked list.

Your messages

Your direct messages and your group chat messages, including messages you sent to other people. Those are removed from the other person's conversation too. If your message was the last one in a conversation, its preview disappears from everyone's list of conversations as well.

Your photos and files

Every image you uploaded is deleted from our storage, not just unlinked from your account. That covers post photos, workout photos, progress photos, your profile picture, your profile banner, and photos you sent in messages. The photo of a group chat stays with the group.

06

If you are a coach

Each of your coaching relationships ends. What you made for each client is deleted: their plans, your notes, their schedule changes, the videos you sent, the calls you set up and the billing records. Your check-in forms and your own custom check-in questions are deleted too.

Some of it stays with your clients, because it is their record:

  • their check-ins and their answers, as they sent them;
  • their phases, under the names you gave them;
  • a frozen copy of the last routine and the last meal plan you gave them.

None of that keeps your name or account attached.

07

What we keep, and for how long

Six kinds of record stay in our database after your account is gone. From the first five your name, your email address and your account ID are removed, with the one exception noted under subscription records, so the record is no longer linked to you or to any person. We keep them because deleting them would break something for someone else, or because we need them for our own records.

Feedback you sent us

If you used Send Feedback in the app, the text of that feedback stays, with your account ID stripped out. We keep it so we do not lose a bug report or a suggestion.

Subscription and purchase records

If you ever subscribed or paid a coach, the record of those payment events stays, with your account ID stripped out of it. Our copy of each event as Stripe or the App Store sent it still contains your account ID or email address. We keep it for tax and accounting.

Referral records

If you used an ambassador code, or your own code was used by someone else, that referral record stays, with the account IDs stripped out.

Payments we made to you

If you were an ambassador and we paid you, the record of that payment stays, with your account ID stripped out. A row here means money actually changed hands, so we keep it for tax and accounting in the same way a business keeps any other payment record.

Group chats you created

If you started a group chat, the group itself stays so the other members keep their conversation. Your messages in it are deleted and your name is removed as its creator.

Custom exercises you made

A custom exercise you created is switched off, not deleted, so that lifts other people logged with it still have a name. It keeps its name and the account ID that made it.

How long: we do not currently put an expiry date on these six. They stay in our database, without your identity attached except where noted above, until we delete them. If you want your feedback and a group chat you created removed as well, email us at support@fizeekapp.com and we will do it by hand. The three money records (purchases, referrals and payments) we have to keep for tax and accounting.

Separately, deleted data can sit in our database provider's encrypted backups for a short time before those backups are overwritten. We cannot reach into a backup to remove one account from it.

08

Copies held by other companies

We want to be straight with you about this, because it is the part most deletion pages leave out.

Some of the companies that help us run Fizeek keep their own copy of a small amount of data about you. Deleting your account does not reach those copies. The deletion clears our database and our storage and cancels your subscriptions, and it does not send a delete instruction to these companies.

Sentry Crash reports. Holds your account ID (reports sent before October 2026 may also hold your email address), your device model and operating system, and the details of any crash you hit, which can include workout values such as the weight and reps of the set you were saving.
PostHog Product analytics. Holds your account ID and email address, and a small fixed set of usage events such as signing up, finishing a workout, or viewing the upgrade screen. It never holds anything you typed.
RevenueCat Subscription management. Holds your account ID and your purchase and subscription history, plus a note of any ambassador code you used.
Stripe Coaching payments. Your coach's Stripe account keeps the record of the payments you made, with the email address you paid with, as any payment processor does. If you are a coach, your own Stripe account keeps your clients' payment records. Deleting your Fizeek account cancels the subscriptions; it does not delete those records.

If you want the Sentry, PostHog and RevenueCat copies deleted too, email support@fizeekapp.com and say so. We will delete your Sentry crash reports and your PostHog events. At RevenueCat we will delete the extra labels we attach to your account, such as a note that you were given Fizeek Pro for free or that you used an ambassador code. Your purchase and subscription history at RevenueCat we keep, for tax and accounting, in the same way as the money records in section 07.

We are working on making this automatic, so that deleting your account clears these copies as well. Until it is, this page tells you where you stand.

09

Deleting the app is not the same thing

Uninstalling Fizeek from your phone does not delete your account. Your data stays on our servers and you can sign back in. To remove your data you have to use one of the routes on this page.
