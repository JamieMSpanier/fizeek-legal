Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Effective Date: (DRAFT)

Draft for review, not in force.

This policy explains how Fizeek LLC (“Fizeek,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses and shares consumer health data, as that term is defined in the Washington My Health My Data Act and similar state laws, such as Nevada's. It covers everyone who uses the Fizeek app and the coaching website at fizeekapp.com: members, clients and coaches. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which covers everything else.

01

The Health Data We Collect

Body measurements: body weight you type in, body weight you give in a check-in answer, other measurements you track, and, on iPhone if you allow it, body weight read from Apple Health.

Training data: your workouts, sets, weights, reps, cardio, personal records and progress.

Nutrition: your meal plan, the meals and foods you tick off, and the water you log.

Health answers: your answers to intake and check-in questions, which can be about sleep, stress, digestion, injuries, conditions, medications or how your body feels.

Photos and videos: progress photos and the photos and videos on your check-ins.

Lab results that you or your coach upload.

Plans and notes about your health: supplement plans and the health notes a coach shares with you.

02

Where It Comes From

From you, when you enter it; from your coach, when they write your plans or upload a lab result; and, on iPhone, from Apple Health, which gives us only your body weight and only with your permission.

03

Why We Use It

To give you the features you use: tracking your training and body, showing your progress, trends and records.

To run coaching you choose: showing your coach your information and delivering their plans and replies to you.

To keep Fizeek working and secure, for example crash reports that can include the workout values you were saving.

We do not use health data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with data brokers.

04

Who Receives It

Your coach, only if you join one. You join by ticking a box that says you agree to share your health and fitness information with your coach. While you are coached, your coach sees what our Privacy Policy lists, including your health answers, progress photos, lab results and the body weights you type in or give in a check-in. Bodyweight read from Apple Health is never shown to a coach. After coaching ends, your former coach sees only what existed up to the day it ended, as it stood that day.

Our service providers, which process it on our behalf and must protect it:

Supabase stores it.

Vercel hosts the coaching website, where your coach sees it.

Sentry receives crash reports, which can include workout values.

PostHog receives a small, fixed set of usage events, such as that you finished a workout.

Expo delivers notifications, whose text can include a coach's reminder or message.

Resend sends our emails, whose contents can include a coach's message to you.

Anthropic receives the words you type to add exercises and the names of the exercises in that workout, and nothing that identifies you.

We may also disclose health data where the law requires it.

05

Your Rights

Know and access: confirm whether we hold your health data and get a copy. In the app's profile settings, tap Request My Data. Your photos, videos and lab files are included as links that download each file; the links work for 7 days.

Delete: delete your account in the app or on the website, which deletes your health data, or ask us.

Withdraw consent: see Section 06.

Know who receives it: the list in Section 04 is complete. Ask us if you want more detail.

To make a request, use the app or email privacy@fizeekapp.com from the email address on your account. We reply within 30 days. If we refuse a request, you can appeal by replying with “Appeal” in the subject; we answer an appeal within 45 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact your state's attorney general.

06

Withdrawing Consent

Sharing with your coach: leave your coach in the app. Your coach stops receiving anything new when your coaching ends. A former coach keeps seeing what existed up to the day it ended.

Apple Health: turn off Fizeek's access in the Health app on your iPhone.

Everything: delete your account.

07

How We Protect It

Health data is encrypted in transit, and our database only lets each person, and the coach they chose, reach their own data. If a breach affects your health data, we will tell you, and the authorities where the law requires, within the time the law sets.

08

Changes and Contact

We will tell you at least 30 days before a material change to this policy takes effect, in the app or by email. The Effective Date at the top shows when it last changed.
