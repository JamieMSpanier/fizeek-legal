Privacy Policy

Effective Date: (DRAFT)

Draft for review, not in force.

Fizeek LLC (“Fizeek,” “we,” “us”) runs the Fizeek app for iPhone and Android and the Fizeek coaching website at fizeekapp.com (together, “Fizeek”). This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. Our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy gives more detail about health data.

01

Who This Policy Covers

This policy covers everyone who uses Fizeek: members who track their own training, clients who work with a coach through Fizeek, and coaches. Where something applies only to clients or only to coaches, we say so.

02

What We Collect

Your account

Your email address, username, name, date of birth, and, if you add them, a profile photo, banner and bio. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive what you choose to share through that service. If you start creating an account and do not finish, what you entered is deleted after 30 days.

Your training and your body

Your workouts and every set you log (exercise, weight, reps, rep techniques, rest), cardio, personal records, routines, goals, body weight and other measurements you track, progress photos, and the notes and setup cues you write.

Apple Health (iPhone only)

If you allow it, your body weight read from Apple Health. Nothing else is read from Apple Health. See Section 04.

Social features

Posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, direct messages and group conversations.

Coaching, if you are a client

What you and your coach exchange: your intake answers, check-in answers (which can include health answers such as sleep, stress, digestion or how part of your body feels), the photos and videos on your check-ins, lab results either of you uploads, the meals and foods you tick off and the water you log, and the programs, meal plans, supplement plans, replies, videos and messages your coach sends you. We also record that you agreed to share your health and fitness information with your coach, and when.

Coaching, if you are a coach

What you create for your clients, your coaching settings and branding, your private notes about each client, and your plan and payment status.

Payments

Coaching payments go through Stripe. We never receive your card number. We keep the price, the schedule, the payment status and the amounts paid between a client and a coach. Fizeek Pro is bought through Apple or Google, which handle the payment.

Agreements

Which version of our Terms and this Privacy Policy you agreed to, when, and whether you agreed in the app or on the website.

App and device information

Crash reports (your account ID, device model, operating system and the details of the crash) and a small, fixed set of usage events, such as signing up or finishing a workout.

03

How We Use It

To run Fizeek: storing your training, showing your progress, running social features and messages, and sending the notifications you have turned on.

To run coaching you choose: showing your coach what Section 05 lists, delivering your coach's plans, replies and reminders, sending invitations on a coach's behalf, and processing coaching payments through Stripe.

To keep Fizeek safe: enforcing our Terms, reviewing reports, and protecting accounts.

To fix and improve Fizeek: crash reports and our small set of usage events.

To contact you: account emails such as password resets, and in-app notifications about activity that involves you.

We do not sell your personal data, we do not share it with data brokers, and Fizeek has no advertising.

04

Apple Health and Apple Watch (iPhone only)

If you allow it, Fizeek reads your body weight from Apple Health and writes the body weight you log in Fizeek back to it. Workouts you log on your Apple Watch are saved to Apple Health so they count toward your Activity rings. Nothing else is read from Apple Health.

Bodyweight read from Apple Health is never shown to a coach. It is used only to show you your own information, and it is never used for advertising or sold.

You can turn this off at any time in the Health app on your iPhone. Your Apple Watch talks only to the Fizeek app on your phone. On Android, Fizeek does not connect to any health app.

05

Coaching: What Your Coach Sees

You join a coach by accepting their invitation and ticking a box that says you agree to share your health and fitness information with them. While you are coached, your coach can see:

your workouts and every set you log, including your training history from before your coaching began, your trends and personal records;

the body weights you type into Fizeek or give in a check-in answer, and the other measurements you track;

your nutrition plan and how closely you followed it;

all of your progress photos;

your intake and check-in answers, including health answers, and the photos and videos on your check-ins;

any lab results either of you uploads;

your email address.

Your coach does not see your direct messages with other members. A coach can keep private notes about you that you do not see; they belong to the coach and are not in your data export. A note your coach shares with you is yours and is in your export.

06

When Coaching Ends

You can leave your coach, and your coach can end your coaching. Either way, future charges stop at once and you both keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. A coaching relationship with no payment ends at once. The Terms explain refunds.

Once coaching has ended, your former coach sees only what existed up to the day it ended, frozen as it was that day, including your plans and your profile settings. They see nothing you log or change afterwards, and no longer your email address. Nothing is deleted when coaching ends. We keep a copy of your training program and your meal plan as they stood that day, and both are in your data export.

07

Deleting an Account That Is Part of Coaching

If you are a client and delete your account, your coaching data is deleted with it, and your former coach no longer sees any of it.

If your coach deletes their account, you keep your own records and everything that was part of your coaching: your workouts and routines, the meals and foods you ticked off, your check-ins and answers with your coach's replies and the videos on them, your check-in forms, your lab results, your training program, meal plan, cardio and supplement plans, the notes your coach shared with you, your calendar, the record of changes to your plans, your calls and your scores. Two things are deleted with their account: your one-to-one conversation with your coach, for both of you, and your billing record with that coach.

08

Messages

Direct messages and group conversations are visible only to the people in them. We do not read them, except where the law requires it or when we review a report from someone in the conversation.

When either person in a one-to-one conversation deletes their account, the whole conversation is deleted for both people, with every message and everything sent in it. When you delete your account, your messages in group chats are removed from those chats.

09

Who We Share It With

We share data only with the services that run Fizeek for us, and only what each one needs. They process it on our behalf and must protect it.

Supabase stores your account and everything you put into Fizeek.

Vercel hosts the coaching website.

Sentry receives crash reports, which can include workout values such as the weight and reps of the set you were saving.

PostHog receives our small, fixed set of usage events, your account ID and your email address. It does not record your screen or your taps.

Expo delivers notifications and receives each notification's text, which can include a message's words or a coach's reminder.

Resend sends our emails and receives each email's address and contents.

Stripe handles coaching payments. When you pay a coach, you pay on the coach's own Stripe account, so your coach can see your email address and your payments there, but not your full card number.

RevenueCat manages Fizeek Pro subscriptions and receives your account ID and purchase history.

Apple and Google handle sign in if you choose them, and Fizeek Pro purchases.

Anthropic receives only the words you type when you add exercises by text, and the names of the exercises already in that workout (Section 10).

Your coach sees what Section 05 lists. Other members see your username, profile photo, bio and what you choose to share. We may also share information when the law requires it.

10

AI Features

You can describe, in your own words, what to add to a workout. We send Anthropic the words you typed, the names and order of the exercises already in that workout and, if you are part-way through it, which exercise you are on. We send nothing that identifies you. We do not store or log what you type, and Anthropic does not use it to train its models.

11

How Long We Keep Data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your personal data from our database and our file storage.

A few records stay after an account is deleted, with your name, email and account ID removed: feedback you sent us, any group chat you created (so its members keep their conversation) and any custom exercise you made (so lifts other people logged with it keep a name). Payment records (subscription and purchase events, coaching payments, ambassador referrals and payments we made) are kept for tax and legal reasons. Our copies of the payment messages Stripe and the app stores send us still contain your account ID or email address.

Deleted data can remain in our database provider's encrypted backups for a short time before they are overwritten. Sentry, PostHog and RevenueCat keep their own copies of the small amount of data listed in Section 09, and Stripe keeps its own payment records. If you ask, we will delete your Sentry crash reports and PostHog events.

12

Your Rights and Your Data

Get a copy. In the app's profile settings, tap Request My Data, once a day. You receive a file with your data, including your coaching data. Your photos, videos and lab files are included as links that download each file; the links work for 7 days. A coach's private notes about you are not included.

Correct it. Edit your profile in the app, or ask us.

Delete it. Delete your account in the app or on the website (see our Delete Your Account page), or ask us.

Object or ask questions. Email us. We reply within 30 days.

California residents have rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what we collect. Residents of Washington, Nevada and other states with consumer health data laws have the rights in our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. Fizeek is not offered in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Fizeek does not respond to Do Not Track signals because it does not track you across other websites or apps.

13

Security

Data is encrypted in transit, our database only lets each person reach their own data, and our provider keeps daily backups. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise that yours never will be breached. If a breach affects your health data, we will tell you as the law requires.

14

Where Data Is Stored

Your data is stored and processed in the United States. If you use Fizeek from another country, your data is transferred to the United States, where the law may differ from yours.

15

Children

Fizeek is not for anyone under 16, and account creation is blocked under 16. In India you must be at least 18. If we learn we have data from someone under the minimum age, we delete it. Tell us if you believe an underage person has an account.

16

Changes to This Policy

We will tell you at least 30 days before a material change takes effect, in the app or by email. When this policy changes materially, you will be asked to read the updated policy and agree to it again before you continue using Fizeek. The Effective Date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
