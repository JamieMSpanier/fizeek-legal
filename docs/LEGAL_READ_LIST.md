## Privacy Policy (`privacy-policy.html`)

**Introduction**
- This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data when you use the Fizeek mobile application or the Fizeek coach website at fizeekapp.com (together, "the App").
**Section 01**
- Sign in with Apple is offered on iPhone and on the coach website, not in the Android app.
- Coaching Information
- If you are coached through Fizeek, we collect what you and your coach exchange: your check-in answers (which can include health answers, such as sleep, stress, digestion or how part of your body feels), the photos and videos you attach to check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, the meals and foods you tick off on your meal plan, the water you log, your answers to your coach's intake form, and the programs, meal plans, supplement plans, replies, videos and messages your coach sends you.
- If you are a coach, we collect what you create for your clients, your coaching settings and branding, and your own notes about each client.
- Coaching payments are handled by Stripe.
- When you pay a coach, or a coach pays for a coach plan, Stripe collects your card details; we never receive or store your card number.
- We keep the price, the billing schedule, the total paid so far, the status and dates of the arrangement, Stripe's reference numbers for it, and a copy of each message Stripe sends us about it, so the App can show them to you and to your coach.
**Section 02**
- Run coaching: showing your coach the information described in Section 06 while you are coached, and afterwards as Section 06 describes, delivering your coach's plans, replies and reminders to you, sending invitation emails on a coach's behalf, and processing coaching payments through Stripe.
**Section 03**
- It receives your device's notification token and the text of each notification sent to you, which can include the words of a message someone sends you, another member's username or, for coaching, your coach's name, a due date or a price.
- It receives the address an email is sent to and the contents of that email, such as feedback you send us, a request to have a training split set up, a coach's invitation (your email address, your coach's name and a link), or a report of content to our moderators, which quotes the reported post, comment or message.
- Coaching payments.
- It receives your email address and card details when you pay a coach or pay for a coach plan, the amount and schedule of each payment, and which coach and client a payment is between.
- A coach who takes payments also gives Stripe the details Stripe needs to pay them.
- Your card number goes to Stripe, never to us.
- Because you pay on your coach's own Stripe account, your coach can see your email address and your payments there, but not your full card number.
- Hosting for the coach website.
- Authentication (if you choose this method; offered on iPhone and on the coach website).
**Section 04**
- It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, your coach included: a coach sees only the body weight you type into Fizeek yourself (Section 06).
- Bodyweight read from Apple Health before this change was stored without a mark saying where it came from, so a coach can still see those earlier readings.
**Section 06**
- If you are coached through Fizeek, your coach can see more of your information than other members can.
- While you are coached, your coach can see your workouts and every set you log, your training history, trends and personal records, your body weight (the weights you type into Fizeek, never the ones read from Apple Health) and other metrics you track, your nutrition plan and how closely you followed it, your progress photos, your check-in answers (including any health answers), the photos and videos on your check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, and your email address.
- Your coach does not see your direct messages with other members.
- Your coach's private notes.
- A coach can keep notes about you that you do not see; they belong to the coach and are not part of your data export.
- A health note your coach shares with you is part of your export.
- When your coaching ends, nothing is deleted.
- Your former coach keeps seeing what you logged up to the day your coaching ended, but nothing you log afterwards, and no longer your email address.
- We also keep a copy of your training program and of your meal plan as they stood on that day; both are included in your data export.
- If your coach deletes their account, your own records stay: your workouts and routines, your check-ins and your answers, the photos and videos you sent, and the meals and foods you ticked off.
- What your coach made for you is deleted with their account: your meal plan, your cardio and supplement plans, their notes and the videos they sent, and your billing details with them.
- We keep a copy of your training program and your meal plan as they stood on that day.
- Deleting your account removes your coaching data too, and your former coach no longer sees any of it in the App.
- Your coach's own Stripe records of your payments are kept by Stripe.
**Section 08**
- Your name, your email address and your account ID are removed from the first five, with one exception: the copies we keep of the payment messages Stripe and the App Store send us still contain your account ID or email address.
- When a coaching relationship ends, nothing is deleted: what your former coach can still see is set out in Section 06.
- Your coaching data stays until you delete your account; what your coach made for you is deleted if they delete theirs (Section 06).
- Stripe keeps its own records of coaching payments.
- Deleting your account cancels any coaching payment or coach plan you have, but Stripe keeps the record of past payments and of the card you saved, under its own privacy policy and the law it must follow; we do not delete those for you.
- Sentry holds crash reports, PostHog holds a small fixed set of usage events, and RevenueCat holds your purchase and subscription history (Stripe's own records are described above).
**Section 09**
- On the coach website a coach deletes their account from the Account card in Settings, which stays open to them even when their account is limited to Billing, and a client who pays a coach deletes theirs from the Account card on their payment page; it asks you to confirm once.
- Deleting your account also cancels any coaching payment you make or receive through Fizeek and any coach plan.
**Section 13**
- You can download a copy of your data once a day in the phone app: go to your profile settings and tap "Request My Data." You will receive a file containing your data in a portable format, including your coaching data (your plans, check-ins, payments and the copies of your program and your meal plan kept when a coaching relationship ends), but not a coach's private notes about you.

## Delete Your Account (`delete-account.html`)

**Introduction**
- You can delete your account in the app, on the Fizeek coaching website, or by emailing us.
**Section 02**
- If you are a coach, sign in to the coaching website, open Settings and find the Account card.
- If you are a client, sign in to the coaching website and open the page where you pay your coach.
- If you do not have a coach, delete your account from the app instead.
- Deleting on the website does exactly what deleting in the app does.
**Section 03**
- If you cannot get into the app or the website, email us at support@fizeekapp.com and ask us to delete your account.
**Section 04**
- Before anything is deleted, we cancel at Stripe every subscription the account has: what you pay a coach, your own Fizeek coach plan if you are a coach, and what each of your clients pays you.
**Section 05**
- Your coaching, if you are a client
- Your coaching links, your check-ins and your answers to them, the videos and photos you sent with them, and everything your coach made for you: your plans, notes, schedules, supplements and lab work.
**Section 06**
- Each of your coaching relationships ends.
- What you made for each client is deleted: their plans, your notes, their schedule changes, the videos you sent, the calls you set up and the billing records.
- Your check-in forms and your own custom check-in questions are deleted too.
- Some of it stays with your clients, because it is their record:
- their check-ins and their answers, as they sent them;
- a frozen copy of the last routine and the last meal plan you gave them.
**Section 07**
- If you ever subscribed or paid a coach, the record of those payment events stays, with your account ID stripped out of it.
- Our copy of each event as Stripe or the App Store sent it still contains your account ID or email address.
**Section 08**
- Coaching payments.
- Your coach's Stripe account keeps the record of the payments you made, with the email address you paid with, as any payment processor does.
- If you are a coach, your own Stripe account keeps your clients' payment records.


## Batch AA (2026-10-30): changed for the owner's and the lawyer's review

What account deletion does to conversations and to a deleted coach's client, reworded to the
rulings of 2026-10-30: deleting any account removes every one-to-one conversation it is part of,
for both people; a client keeps everything else that was part of their coaching when the coach
deletes their account; the billing record with that coach is deleted. App migration 713, not yet
applied. Only these sentences changed.

**Privacy Policy, Section 06**
- Was: If your coach deletes their account, your own records stay: your workouts and routines, your check-ins and your answers, the photos and videos you sent, and the meals and foods you ticked off.
- Now: If your coach deletes their account, you keep your own records and everything that was part of your coaching: your workouts and routines, the meals and foods you ticked off, your check-ins and your answers with your coach's replies and the videos on them, your check-in forms, your training program, meal plan, cardio and supplement plans, the notes your coach shared with you, your calendar, the record of changes to your plans, your calls and your scores.
- Was: What your coach made for you is deleted with their account: your meal plan, your cardio and supplement plans, their notes and the videos they sent, and your billing details with them.
- Now: Two things are deleted with their account: your one-to-one conversation with your coach, for both of you, including your own messages and everything sent in it, and your billing record with that coach.

**Privacy Policy, Section 08**
- Was: This includes your profile information, workout data, posts, comments, and messages (including messages you sent to other users, which are removed from their conversations) as well as the photos and images you uploaded, which are deleted from our storage (the photo of a group chat stays with the group).
- Now: This includes your profile information, workout data, posts, comments, and messages (every one-to-one conversation you are part of is deleted for both people, with every message either of you sent in it and everything sent with them, and the messages you sent in group chats are removed from those chats) as well as the photos and images you uploaded, which are deleted from our storage (the photo of a group chat stays with the group).
- Was: Your coaching data stays until you delete your account; what your coach made for you is deleted if they delete theirs (Section 06).
- Now: Your coaching data stays until you delete your account, and stays if your coach deletes theirs, except your one-to-one conversation with them and your billing record with them (Section 06).

**Terms & Conditions, Section 08**
- Was: If your coach deletes their account, your own records stay and what they made for you is deleted, as our Privacy Policy describes.
- Now: If your coach deletes their account, you keep everything that was part of your coaching except your one-to-one conversation with them, which is deleted for both of you, and your billing record with them, as our Privacy Policy describes.

**Delete Your Account, Section 05 (Your messages)**
- Was: Your direct messages and your group chat messages, including messages you sent to other people. Those are removed from the other person's conversation too. If your message was the last one in a conversation, its preview disappears from everyone's list of conversations as well.
- Now: Every one-to-one conversation you are part of, for both people: every message you or the other person sent in it, and everything sent with them, such as photos, videos, polls and workout invitations. Your group chat messages are removed from the group. If your message was the last one in a group chat, its preview disappears from everyone's list of conversations as well.

**Delete Your Account, Section 06 (If you are a coach)**
- Was: Each of your coaching relationships ends. What you made for each client is deleted: their plans, your notes, their schedule changes, the videos you sent, the calls you set up and the billing records. Your check-in forms and your own custom check-in questions are deleted too.
- Was: Some of it stays with your clients, because it is their record: their check-ins and their answers, as they sent them; their phases, under the names you gave them; a frozen copy of the last routine and the last meal plan you gave them.
- Now: Each of your coaching relationships ends. Everything that was part of each client's coaching stays with that client, because it is their record: their check-ins and their answers, as they sent them, with your replies and the videos on them, and the check-in forms they were sent; their training program, meal plan, cardio and supplement plans, and a frozen copy of the last routine and the last meal plan you gave them; their phases, under the names you gave them; the notes you shared with them, their calendar, the record of changes to their plans, the calls you set up with them, and their scores.
- Now: Your one-to-one conversation with each person, a client or anyone else, is deleted for both of you, including their messages and everything sent in it. What is only yours is deleted: your private notes about clients, your settings, the check-in forms and videos no client was given, and the billing records with your clients.

## Batch AB (2026-10-30): changed for the owner's and the lawyer's review

What a coach can see from before coaching, reworded to ruling R1 of 2026-10-30: a coach sees the
client's full logged history from before coaching too, and all progress photos, with two limits:
a body weight dated before October 30, 2026 is never shown to a coach, and a weight read from Apple
Health never is. App migration 715, not yet applied. Only these sentences changed. The client is
also told on the screen where they accept an invite: "Your coach will see your workout history and
all your progress photos."

**Privacy Policy, Section 06**
- Was: While you are coached, your coach can see your workouts and every set you log, your training history, trends and personal records, your body weight (the weights you type into Fizeek, never the ones read from Apple Health) and other metrics you track, your nutrition plan and how closely you followed it, your progress photos, your check-in answers (including any health answers), the photos and videos on your check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, and your email address.
- Now: While you are coached, your coach can see your workouts and every set you log, your training history (including what you logged before your coaching began), trends and personal records, your body weight (the weights you type into Fizeek dated October 30, 2026 or later, never the ones read from Apple Health) and other metrics you track, your nutrition plan and how closely you followed it, all your progress photos, your check-in answers (including any health answers), the photos and videos on your check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, and your email address.

- Was: While you are coached, your coach can see the information listed in our Privacy Policy, including your workouts, nutrition, check-ins, progress photos and, while your coaching is active, your email address.
- Now: While you are coached, your coach can see the information listed in our Privacy Policy, including your workouts and training history from before your coaching began, nutrition, check-ins, all your progress photos and, while your coaching is active, your email address.

**Not changed, for the owner's decision: Privacy Policy, Section 04**
- Still reads: Bodyweight read from Apple Health before this change was stored without a mark saying where it came from, so a coach can still see those earlier readings.
