Fizeek is operated by Fizeek LLC ("Fizeek," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data when you use the Fizeek mobile application or the Fizeek coach website at fizeekapp.com (together, "the App").

01

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide you with a functional, personalized workout tracking and social experience.

Account Information

When you create an account, we collect your email address, username, full name (optional), date of birth, and optionally a profile photo and bio. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the information you choose to share through that sign-in service. Sign in with Apple is offered on iPhone and on the coach website, not in the Android app. If you begin creating an account but do not finish, the answers you entered are stored temporarily and automatically deleted after 30 days.

Workout & Fitness Data

This includes exercises, sets, reps, weight, rep techniques, rest times, workout duration, cardio activities, personal records, workout splits, body weight entries, and any setup cues you create. This data is central to the App's purpose and is stored to track your progress over time.

Apple Health Data (iPhone only)

On iPhone, if you grant permission, we read your bodyweight from Apple Health and store it with your other body weight entries. We do not read any other Health data. Workouts you record on your Apple Watch are written to Apple Health but are not collected from it. This does not apply on Android: Fizeek does not connect to any health app there, and reads no health data from your phone.

Social & User-Generated Content

If you use social features, we collect posts (including text and photos), comments, likes, follows, direct messages, and group conversation content. Your username, profile photo, and public posts are visible to other users.

Coaching Information

If you are coached through Fizeek, we collect what you and your coach exchange: your check-in answers (which can include health answers, such as sleep, stress, digestion or how part of your body feels), the photos and videos you attach to check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, the meals and foods you tick off on your meal plan, the water you log, your answers to your coach's intake form, and the programs, meal plans, supplement plans, replies, videos and messages your coach sends you. If you are a coach, we collect what you create for your clients, your coaching settings and branding, and your own notes about each client.

Payment Information

Coaching payments are handled by Stripe. When you pay a coach, or a coach pays for a coach plan, Stripe collects your card details; we never receive or store your card number. We keep the price, the billing schedule, the total paid so far, the status and dates of the arrangement, Stripe's reference numbers for it, and a copy of each message Stripe sends us about it, so the App can show them to you and to your coach.

Device & Technical Data

We collect your push notification token (to send you notifications), device type and operating system, your device's region setting (to apply the correct minimum age requirement), crash and error reports to help us diagnose and fix issues, and your in-app search history to power search features. We also collect limited product analytics to understand how the App is used and improve it: a small, fixed set of events covering signup, onboarding progress, workout completion, paywall views, purchases and trials, and use of workout-building features. These events never include what you type.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Provide the App's core features: storing your workouts, tracking your progress, managing your split, powering social features like posts and messaging, and delivering push notifications you've opted into.

Improve the App: analyzing crash reports and limited usage analytics to fix bugs, improve performance, and develop new features.

Communicate with you: sending transactional emails related to your account (such as password resets) and in-app notifications about activity relevant to you (such as when someone comments on your post).

Run coaching: showing your coach the information described in Section 06 while you are coached, and afterwards as Section 06 describes, delivering your coach's plans, replies and reminders to you, sending invitation emails on a coach's behalf, and processing coaching payments through Stripe.

Maintain safety: enforcing our Terms of Service, responding to reports of abusive content or messages, and protecting the security of the App and its users.

03

Third-Party Services

We use a limited number of third-party services to operate the App. These providers process data on our behalf and are contractually obligated to protect it.

Supabase Database hosting, authentication and file storage. This is where your account lives. It holds everything you put into the App: your profile, workouts, body weight, posts, comments, messages and photos.
Sentry Crash reporting & error monitoring. It receives your user ID (crash reports sent before October 2026 may also hold your email address), your device model and operating system, and the details of a crash. Those details can include workout values, such as the weight and reps of the set you were saving.
PostHog Limited product analytics (a small, fixed set of usage events). It receives those events, your user ID and your email address. We switch off its location lookup, and it does not record your screen or your taps.
Expo Push notification delivery & app updates. It receives your device's notification token and the text of each notification sent to you, which can include the words of a message someone sends you, another member's username or, for coaching, your coach's name, a due date or a price.
Resend Transactional email delivery. It receives the address an email is sent to and the contents of that email, such as feedback you send us, a request to have a training split set up, a coach's invitation (your email address, your coach's name and a link), or a report of content to our moderators, which quotes the reported post, comment or message.
Stripe Coaching payments. It receives your email address and card details when you pay a coach or pay for a coach plan, the amount and schedule of each payment, and which coach and client a payment is between. A coach who takes payments also gives Stripe the details Stripe needs to pay them. Your card number goes to Stripe, never to us. Because you pay on your coach's own Stripe account, your coach can see your email address and your payments there, but not your full card number.
Vercel Hosting for the coach website. It receives the requests your browser makes to fizeekapp.com, including your IP address.
RevenueCat Subscription management & purchase validation. It receives your user ID and your purchase and subscription history. It never receives your card details; those stay with Apple or Google.
Apple Sign In Authentication (if you choose this method; offered on iPhone and on the coach website). It receives the sign-in request. We receive back whatever you choose to share.
Google Sign In Authentication (if you choose this method). It receives the sign-in request. We receive back whatever you choose to share.
Anthropic AI workout building. It receives the words you type when you add exercises by text, the names and order of the exercises already in that workout, and which exercise you are on if you are part-way through it; nothing that identifies you (Section 15). We do not store what you type and we do not log it, and it is not used to train models.

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. We do not share your data with data brokers. The App contains no advertising and no advertising or tracking SDKs, and we do not share your data with anyone for advertising purposes.

04

Apple Health and Apple Watch (iPhone only)

This section applies on iPhone. On Android, Fizeek does not connect to any health app and there is no watch app, so none of it applies to you.

If you allow it, Fizeek reads your bodyweight from Apple Health and writes bodyweight you log in Fizeek back to it. Workouts logged from your Apple Watch are saved to Apple Health, including duration and estimated calories, so they count toward your Activity rings. Health data is only used to show you your own information inside Fizeek. Bodyweight read from Apple Health is saved to your Fizeek account so it appears in your history, the same as bodyweight you type in yourself. It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone, your coach included: a coach sees only the body weight you type into Fizeek yourself (Section 06). Bodyweight read from Apple Health before this change was stored without a mark saying where it came from, so a coach can still see those earlier readings. You can turn this off at any time in the Health app on your iPhone. Your Apple Watch talks only to the Fizeek app on your phone; it never contacts our servers directly.

05

Social Features & Public Content

Fizeek includes social features that allow you to share content with other users. When you use these features:

Your username, profile photo, and bio are visible to all users of the App, regardless of your privacy settings.

Profile privacy: You can make your profile private in Settings. When your profile is private, your posts, workout stats, and other content are visible only to your approved followers. Non-followers can still see your username, profile photo, and bio, and can request to follow you. When your profile is public, your posts are visible to all users.

Direct messages and group conversations are visible only to the participants in that conversation. If your profile is private, only your approved followers can send you direct messages. We do not monitor or read your private messages. However, we may access message content if required by law or when reviewing a report submitted by a participant in the conversation.

Workout data is private to you unless you explicitly choose to share it via a post.

We will never use your photos, posts, or other content in advertising or promotional materials without your separate, explicit permission.

You can block other users and report posts, profiles, and direct messages at any time through the App.

06

Coaching

If you are coached through Fizeek, your coach can see more of your information than other members can. This section says what, and for how long.

While you are coached, your coach can see your workouts and every set you log, your training history (including what you logged before your coaching began), trends and personal records, your body weight (the weights you type into Fizeek dated October 30, 2026 or later, never the ones read from Apple Health) and other metrics you track, your nutrition plan and how closely you followed it, all your progress photos, your check-in answers (including any health answers), the photos and videos on your check-ins, any lab results either of you uploads, and your email address. Your coach does not see your direct messages with other members.

Your coach's private notes. A coach can keep notes about you that you do not see; they belong to the coach and are not part of your data export. A health note your coach shares with you is part of your export.

When your coaching ends, nothing is deleted. Your former coach keeps seeing what you logged up to the day your coaching ended, but nothing you log afterwards, and no longer your email address. They also keep seeing your plans and your profile settings, and if you change those later, they see the change. We also keep a copy of your training program and of your meal plan as they stood on that day; both are included in your data export.

If your coach deletes their account, you keep your own records and everything that was part of your coaching: your workouts and routines, the meals and foods you ticked off, your check-ins and your answers with your coach's replies and the videos on them, your check-in forms, your training program, meal plan, cardio and supplement plans, the notes your coach shared with you, your calendar, the record of changes to your plans, your calls and your scores. Two things are deleted with their account: your one-to-one conversation with your coach, for both of you, including your own messages and everything sent in it, and your billing record with that coach. We keep a copy of your training program and your meal plan as they stood on that day.

Deleting your account removes your coaching data too, and your former coach no longer sees any of it in the App. Your coach's own Stripe records of your payments are kept by Stripe.

08

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. Workout history, posts, messages, and profile information are stored so you can access them whenever you use the App.

If you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our systems. This includes your profile information, workout data, posts, comments, and messages (every one-to-one conversation you are part of is deleted for both people, with every message either of you sent in it and everything sent with them, and the messages you sent in group chats are removed from those chats) as well as the photos and images you uploaded, which are deleted from our storage (the photo of a group chat stays with the group).

Six kinds of record stay in our database after your account is gone. They are: feedback you sent us through the App; the record of your subscription and purchase events; any ambassador referral record involving you; the record of any ambassador payment we made to you; any group chat you created, which stays so its other members keep their conversation; and any custom exercise you made, which is switched off but kept so that lifts other people logged with it still have a name. Your name, your email address and your account ID are removed from the first five, with one exception: the copies we keep of the payment messages Stripe and the App Store send us still contain your account ID or email address. We do not currently put an expiry date on these six. The three money records (purchases, referrals and payments) we keep for tax and accounting. Your feedback and a group chat you created we will remove on request, by hand; a custom exercise stays so other people's logged lifts keep its name.

When a coaching relationship ends, nothing is deleted: what your former coach can still see is set out in Section 06. Your coaching data stays until you delete your account, and stays if your coach deletes theirs, except your one-to-one conversation with them and your billing record with them (Section 06).

Stripe keeps its own records of coaching payments. Deleting your account cancels any coaching payment or coach plan you have, but Stripe keeps the record of past payments and of the card you saved, under its own privacy policy and the law it must follow; we do not delete those for you.

Three of our providers keep their own copy of a small amount of data about you, and deleting your account does not reach those copies. Sentry holds crash reports, PostHog holds a small fixed set of usage events, and RevenueCat holds your purchase and subscription history (Stripe's own records are described above). Account deletion clears our database and our storage; it does not send a delete instruction to these three. If you ask us to, we will delete your Sentry crash reports and your PostHog events, and at RevenueCat we will delete the extra labels attached to your account, such as a note that you were given Fizeek Pro for free or that you used an ambassador code. Your purchase and subscription history at RevenueCat we keep, for tax and accounting, for the same reason as the money records above. We are working on making the rest of this automatic.

Separately, deleted data can sit in our database provider's encrypted backups for a short time before those backups are overwritten, and we may retain limited data where required by law or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App. Open the Profile tab, tap the pencil icon in the top right to open Edit Profile, scroll to the section headed Account, and tap Delete Account. You will be asked to confirm twice, including typing DELETE. Account deletion removes your profile, workout data, posts, messages, and uploaded photos from the App and our storage. It takes effect immediately. This action is permanent and cannot be undone. On the coach website a coach deletes their account from the Account card in Settings, which stays open to them even when their account is limited to Billing, and a client who pays a coach deletes theirs from the Account card on their payment page; it asks you to confirm once. Deleting your account also cancels any coaching payment you make or receive through Fizeek and any coach plan.

If you are unable to delete your account through the App, you may contact us at the email address listed below and we will process your request. Please write from the email address on the account, so we can confirm the request is really from you.

Our Account Deletion page sets out the in-app steps, what is deleted, and the records described in Section 08 that are kept or held by a provider.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS. Our database employs row-level security policies to ensure users can only access their own data. Our infrastructure is hosted by Supabase on secure, managed servers with automatic daily backups.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data, including:

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-App tools described above or contact us at the email address below. We will reply to requests within 30 days.

If you are a resident of California, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect and the right to opt out of the sale of your data. As stated above, we do not sell personal data.

The App is currently not offered in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. If we expand availability to those regions in the future, we will update this policy to reflect the applicable requirements.

Do Not Track: The App does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals because, as a mobile application, we do not track users across third-party websites or services.

15

AI Features

The App includes a feature that lets you describe, in your own words, what you want added to a workout, for example “add a hamstring curl and a calf raise”. To do this, we send your request to Anthropic, which operates the AI model that interprets it.

What we send: the words you typed, the names and order of the exercises already in the workout you are looking at, and, if you are part-way through a workout, which exercise you are on. That is everything.

What we do not send: your name, email address, account identifier, workout history, past or planned weights and reps, your set and rep preferences, body weight, photos, or anything else identifying you.

What we do not keep: we do not store what you type. It is not written to our database, it is not written to our logs, and it is not included in crash reports. We keep only a count of how many requests you have made, so that we can apply a daily limit.

Anthropic does not use this data to train their models. You can use every other part of the App without using this feature.
